Be it known that 1, WILLIAM D. MIDDLE- BROOK, a citizen of the United States, residing at Waterbury, in the county of New Haven and State of Connecticut, have invented certain new and useful Improvements in Machines for Making Wire Paper-Clips, of which the following is a specification.
